Dino Math Game

Run around with the T-Rex with WASD or by clicking on things and eat the correct numbers.


Lunar Lander Math Game

Use the keyboard, touch, or mouse controls to guide the lander to land on the correct answer. Try not to land too hard or you might crash.


Snowboarding Math Game

Use the mouse to guide the snowboarder to the correct math equations. You will get points for skipping the wrong math equations. Avoid trees!


Math Murder Math Game

Use the keyboard and mouse to move around and kill the robot that has the correct answer on it's face. If you take too long it will attempt to murder you.
