LUNAR LANDER MATH

Objective

Land your lunar lander on the correct answer to math questions. The faster you land correctly, the higher your score!

Controls

Keyboard:

  • Spacebar - Upward thrust (slows descent)
  • W/A/S/D - Lateral thrust (forward/left/backward/right)

Mouse/Touch:

  • Click/Touch and Hold - Activates thrusters
  • Move - Tilt lander to control direction

Scoring

  • Base Points: 100 maximum per question
  • Time Bonus: Decreases by 2 points/second (min 10)
  • Hard Landing: -20 points if landing speed > 2
  • Wrong Answer: 0 points, position resets

Vertical Speed Indicator (Left Side)

  • Green - Safe landing speed
  • Orange - Caution
  • Red - Hard landing penalty

Tips

  • Use spacebar for vertical control, WASD for positioning
  • Watch the VSI to ensure soft landings
  • Land with descent speed under 2 to avoid penalties
  • The bonus timer shows your current time bonus
