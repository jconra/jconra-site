How they grow
The 16 kinds

patch: holds stretches of ground by itself · clump: small family groups · loner: here and there. The small grey word is the ground it likes. Only shows just that kind.

Tree families

Show

By patch: each spot in the colour of the patch kind that wins it (the dots by the kinds' names), strong where one kind holds it alone, grey where several are close and their plants mix, dark brown where the ground is bare. Thick and thin: bright where plants grow thick, dark where they thin out.

The ground's colours: green grass, yellow dry, brown steep ground and paths, grey stony shore, blue water. Trees are simple stand-ins (cones: pines; balls: ash, aspen, oak), tinted as in the Terrain Lab.
