The versions

raw: as Tripo made it. weld: corners closer than 0.2 mm merged (Merge by Distance); Tripo splits every corner along texture seams and creases, so this halves the corners, but on its own it blackens leaves, because Tripo's faces don't all point the same way. fixed: weld, then every face of a surface turned to point the same way and shaded smooth except across creases sharper than 35°. planar: fixed, then faces lying flat together merged (Decimate Planar, 5°, keeping texture seams). tri600 / tri300: planar, then shrunk toward 600 / 300 triangles (Decimate Collapse).

A plant made of many separate little pieces (lots of loose leaves or flower heads) won't shrink much below a thousand or so: shrinking can't join pieces that aren't connected.

About the files

Each version of a sheet is one glTF with all nine plants, sharing one 2K texture. Made by labs/plants/process.py (Blender 4.0, run from the command line) from the sheets as Tripo exported them.
