Intro

Keys

The camera, where it looks, the chair and how much he faces the camera, at each moment. Pick a key and move it; the scene jumps there so you see it. Add a key at the current time, then Copy settings when it is right and I will bake it in.

In free look, place the camera where you want it, pick a key and press "Use this view": the key takes the camera's place and what it looks at. Changing a key's sliders from free look does the same first, so the camera stays where you put it. Editing holds the timeline; scrolling the scene lets it play on.
