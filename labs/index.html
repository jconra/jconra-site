Labs

Three.js experiments, each self-contained. They were written at different times against different versions of the library, so the version each one pins is listed with it.

Station Lab

A kilometre-scale space station whose outer ring spins for gravity, with hull lamps, bloom, depth of field, lens flare and film grain to tune. This page runs on WebGL1, the version that works everywhere.

three 0.158WebGL1post-processingportfolio

Station Lab r186

The same Station Lab on three.js 0.186 and WebGL2, for comparison: environment lighting and multisampled effects that WebGL1 cannot do.

three 0.186WebGL2compare

Rig Lab

Pose any rigged glTF in the browser, measure what each bone actually drives once skinning is applied, and search for the pose that reaches furthest. Drop your own .glb in.

three 0.158skinned glTFtool

Sound Lab

The modular synth behind RMRF's sound. Patch oscillators, filters, noise, envelopes and LFOs together with cables in a 3D node editor, fire the patch to hear it, and export it to the game. No samples: every engine and gunshot is synthesised live.

three 0.160web audiormrf tool

Asset Designer

Builds the buildings and props that go onto RMRF maps. Shape them from primitives, set materials and damage behaviour, and measure each one's footprint on the map grid before it is written into the game's asset manifest.

rmrf toolmodellingrmrfbase.com

Character Lab

Three Tripo generations of Jacob on plinths, sharing one skeleton and set of animations. Orbit around them, jump to a face close-up, switch between walking and sitting, and tone down the hair.

three 0.158glTFriggingcompare

Character Lab r186

The Character Lab on the latest three.js, to compare against 0.158. Adds tone mapping choices (ACES, AgX, Neutral), exposure and environment light. Needs WebGL2.

three 0.186glTFcompare

Hoodie Lab

Two Tripo generations of the city skyline hoodie side by side under a spotlight. Move the mouse to spin them and compare the two.

three 0.158glTFcompare

Boxing

Stephen versus a chimpanzee. Watch the chimp's tell, dodge out of the way, then hit back. Beat it and round two brings on a warrior bug that cannot be beaten.

three 0.158skinned glTFplayable

Bug Lab

Two rigged bugs wander an arena. When one catches the other in its sight cone it squares up, rears as a threat display, then lunges. Five baked animations each.

three 0.158skinned glTFwebgl 1 safe

Raycast Map

An SVG map of the United States in 3D, with a raycaster picking out whichever region sits under the cursor.

three 0.156raycastingsvg

Tree

A procedurally grown tree — branches recursing down to leaf geometry. Leaf count, leaf size and age are live sliders, so it regrows as you drag.

three r99procedural
